# Changelog

All notable changes to the Defensive Philosophies Reference Guide are documented in this file.
The project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [1.0.0] - 2026-09-17

### Added
- **Core Doctrinal Analyses**:
  - `docs/01-fabian-strategy.md`: In-depth study of Quintus Fabius Maximus Verrucosus, the Second Punic War, the Ager Falernus bottleneck, Cannae aftermath, and modern adaptations (George Washington, Vo Nguyen Giap).
  - `docs/02-defense-in-depth.md`: Elastic defense theory, three-zone defensive architecture (disruption, battle, reserve), Roman frontier *limitanei/comitatenses*, WWI Hindenburg Line (*Siegfriedstellung* and Fritz von Lossberg's doctrine), and the Battle of Kursk (Operation Citadel, 8-belt Soviet defense).
  - `docs/03-fortified-lines-and-bastions.md`: Bastion fortification systems, Sébastien Le Prestre de Vauban's *Pré-Carré*, the Lines of Torres Vedras (Viscount Wellington and Richard Fletcher), and the Maginot Line (tactical invulnerability vs. operational bypass).
  - `docs/04-scorched-earth-and-strategic-space.md`: Spatial attrition, logistics exhaustion, Scythian campaign of 513 BC, and the Russian 1812 Patriotic War against Napoleon (Barclay de Tolly, Kutuzov, Borodino, Moscow).
  - `docs/05-chokepoint-and-natural-barrier-defense.md`: Force multiplication through terrain constriction, the Battle of Thermopylae (480 BC, Phocian Wall), and Julius Caesar's double circumvallation and contravallation at Alesia (52 BC).
  - `docs/06-strategic-matrix-and-modern-applications.md`: Cross-doctrine comparative matrix, Clausewitzian transition dynamics (defense-to-counter-offensive), and modern application blueprints for cybersecurity, sports, and organizational resilience.
  - `docs/07-geographic-atlas.md`: Topographical profiles, OpenStreetMap identifiers, and coordinates for 12 landmark defensive sites.
- **Geospatial & Structured Data**:
  - `data/defensive_sites.json`: Machine-readable catalog containing coordinates, historical eras, commanders, doctrines, and operational notes.
  - `data/defensive_sites.geojson`: RFC 7946 compliant GeoJSON FeatureCollection for immediate integration into GIS systems, interactive maps, and tracking dashboards.
- **Root Documentation**:
  - `README.md`: Complete guide overview, doctrinal taxonomy, quick reference matrix, geographic index, and team version-pulling workflow.
